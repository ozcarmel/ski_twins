BEFORE YOU DROP IN

A few good
questions.

Are these firsthand resort reviews?+

The first destination pages are planning guides based on linked official resort information. They are clearly labelled. Liron and Oz’s firsthand reviews, rental recommendations and accommodation picks will be added separately, with visit dates and any relevant disclosures.

Can I book a holiday through Ski Twins?+

Ski Twins helps you research and plan your own trip. The budget builder is an estimate using your inputs. You book directly with the providers you choose; we do not take bookings or payments.

Is this useful for first-time skiers?+

Yes. Start with our DIY guide, compare learning areas and ski-school options, and include lessons and suitable equipment in your budget. A resort’s total piste length tells you less than whether its terrain suits your group.

Where can I follow the twins?+

We’re preparing Ski Twins for Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. Verified profile links will appear here when the channels launch.
