Alpine destination guides

Nine destinations in Italy, Austria and France. Each guide explains the selection, terrain and evening atmosphere, with sources and a link to build your trip.

How were these destinations selected?

France uses the top three in Alti-Mag’s published 2025/26 skier-day ranking. Reporting units include Les Arcs with Peisey-Vallandry and Val Thorens with Orelle. La Plagne is a rounded figure supplied by the resort. Austria uses municipal winter overnight stays for Sölden, Saalbach-Hinterglemm and Ischgl, with current 2025/26 figures. Italy is an editorial shortlist of Livigno, Val Gardena and Campiglio; a national top three by skier volume has not been verified.

Skier days count each person on each day they ski, not unique people in a season. Overnight stays include non-skiers. Daily first entries differ from repeated lift rides. Do not compare unlike metrics or geographic reporting units.
