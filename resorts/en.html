Ski destination guides

Two holiday tracks by ski-area size: up to 200 km for value comparisons and over 200 km for big-area holidays. Guides include area size, sources and après-ski.

How were these destinations selected?

Area size determines the track regardless of country: up to 200 km in value, strictly over 200 km in big areas. Separate resorts on regional passes are not summed. Size does not guarantee a low price. The shortlist is editorial, not a verified national ranking; available demand evidence is shown separately.

Skier days count each person on each day they ski, not unique people in a season. Overnight stays include non-skiers. Daily first entries differ from repeated lift rides. Do not compare unlike metrics or geographic reporting units.
