Before you book

  • Choose your village and accommodation for access to suitable terrain and your ski school.
  • Check lift-pass coverage: local and linked-area access can require different tickets.
  • Compare the whole trip: accommodation, transfers, equipment, lessons and going out, not just the lift pass.
  • Check venue hours and your route home. If quiet matters, check how close your room is to bars.
